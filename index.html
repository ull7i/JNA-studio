    About the studio

    The name Offreel comes from old printing, when layers slip slightly out of place and the picture comes out alive instead of perfect. We prefer an honest shot to a staged one.

    We work with individuals, families, shops and events, and every project starts with one question: what do you want to remember years from now?

    Honest shots
    We guide gently and leave room for the moment to happen.
    Considered light
    We use natural light whenever we can and add lighting where it's needed.
    Agreed delivery
    We settle the delivery date and file formats with you before the session.
